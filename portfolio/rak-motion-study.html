Motion that earns
its place.

Depth, not decoration.

The background reacts to pointer movement and scroll velocity, but remains secondary to the content. The interaction is felt before it is noticed.

Timing as hierarchy.

Type, cards and supporting detail enter at different velocities so the eye always knows what matters first.

Responsive by design.

The system collapses cleanly on small screens instead of simply shrinking desktop choreography.

Performance-aware.

One lightweight WebGL scene, CSS-native layout, minimal DOM mutation, requestAnimationFrame rendering and no oversized image payload.
